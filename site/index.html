How deals get on the board

A Dallas price, or it doesn’t post

Every fly + stay deal shows a price for flying out of DFW or Love Field, or the seller’s stated Dallas add-on. Headline prices from other cities don’t count.

Per person, two sharing

Package prices are per traveler for two adults in one room, as advertised on the day the deal was found. What’s included varies by seller and is listed on each ticket.

Drive-to prices are per night

Cabin and hotel prices are the seller’s starting nightly rate for the whole cabin or room. Some deals show a discount or package instead, because the seller posts the offer but not the rate.

Checked every morning

A daily check re-confirms prices and retires deals that sold out or expired, and a full search for new deals runs every Monday. Deals past their book-by or travel dates hide themselves right away.

Compare in one tap

Each listing links to the same trip on Expedia, or to cabins on Vrbo, with sample dates inside the deal’s window. Prices there are live; the board shows what the seller advertised.

Book with the seller

Links open the seller’s own page. Prices move daily, so confirm the final price there. Some Travelzoo offers need a Travelzoo Club membership to book.
