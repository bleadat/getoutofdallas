    The fine print, upfront

    How deals get on the board

    1. 01

      A Dallas price, or it doesn’t post

      Every fly + stay deal shows a price for flying out of DFW or Love Field, or the seller’s stated Dallas add-on. Headline prices from other cities don’t count.

    2. 02

      Per person, two sharing

      Package prices are per traveler for two adults in one room, as advertised the day the deal was found.

    3. 03

      Drive-to prices are per night

      The seller’s starting nightly rate for the whole cabin or room — or the discount, when no rate is posted.

    4. 04

      Checked every morning

      A daily check re-confirms prices and retires sold-out deals; a full search for new ones runs every Monday. Deals past their book-by or travel dates hide themselves right away.

    5. 05

      Compare in one tap

      Each listing links to the same trip on Expedia, or cabins on Vrbo, with sample dates inside the deal’s window. Prices there are live; the board shows what the seller advertised.

    6. 06

      Book with the seller

      Links open the seller’s own page. Prices move daily, so confirm the final price there. Some Travelzoo offers need a Travelzoo Club membership to book.
