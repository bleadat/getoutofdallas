01About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Viz Studio LLC (“we,” “us” or “our”), which owns and operates GetoutofDallas.com, may collect, use and share information when you visit GetoutofDallas.com, open our emails or otherwise interact with us (together, the “Site”). It’s part of our Terms of Service.

By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the Site.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

If you contact us, sign up for emails or alerts, or otherwise send us information, we collect what you provide, such as your email address and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Site, we and the companies that help us run it may automatically collect information such as:

  • device and browser type, operating system, language and screen size;
  • IP address, and general location (such as city or region) based on it;
  • pages you view, searches and filters you use, deals and links you click, and how long you stay;
  • the website, ad or email that brought you here, and the date and time of your visit;
  • cookie IDs and similar online identifiers.

Information saved in your browser

Some features save information in your own browser, such as deals you save. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Information from partners

We may receive information from partners such as analytics providers, ad networks and affiliate programs, for example reports about clicks and bookings that came from the Site.

03Cookies and similar technologies

We and third parties, such as analytics providers, ad networks and affiliate partners, may use cookies, pixels, tags, local storage and similar technologies. They help remember your preferences, understand how the Site is used, count clicks on deals, prevent fraud, and show and measure ads, including ads based on your interests.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. If you do, some parts of the Site may not work as intended.

04How we use information

We may use information to:

  • run, maintain and improve the Site and the deals on it;
  • understand how visitors use the Site and which deals they find useful;
  • show, personalize and measure ads;
  • track referrals and commissions with travel sellers and affiliate partners;
  • send emails you’ve signed up for and reply to your messages;
  • protect the Site and its visitors, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems;
  • comply with the law and enforce our Terms.

05Analytics

We may use Google Analytics and similar tools to understand how visitors find and use the Site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, the site that referred you, and your device, browser and general location, and gives us reports about Site activity.

To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. To opt out, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

06Advertising

We may show ads on the Site from Google and other third-party ad networks. These companies may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this Site and other websites, and to measure how ads perform.

When ads appear on the Site, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of many other companies’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices and on the Network Advertising Initiative’s opt-out page. Opting out doesn’t remove ads; the ads you see will just be less tailored to you.

If we work with other ad networks, we’ll list them here with links to their opt-out options.

07Travel sellers and other sites

The Site links to airlines, hotels, booking sites and other travel sellers. When you click a link, you leave the Site, and the seller’s own privacy policy and terms cover anything you do there, including bookings, payments and accounts. We don’t control those sites and aren’t responsible for their privacy practices.

Links may include tracking codes so a seller or affiliate network can tell that you came from GetoutofDallas.com.

08How we share information

We may share information:

  • with service providers that help run the Site, such as hosting, content delivery, security, analytics, email and advertising providers;
  • with advertising and affiliate partners, as described above;
  • when we believe the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of us, our visitors or others;
  • as part of a sale, merger or other transfer of all or part of our business or the Site;
  • with your consent or at your direction.

We may also share aggregated or de-identified information that can’t reasonably be used to identify you.

09How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, unless the law requires or allows us to keep it longer. Service providers and partners keep information according to their own policies.

10How we protect information

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website, email or online service is completely secure. We can’t guarantee the security of any information you send us or that’s collected through the Site.

11Your choices and rights

  • Cookies: block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Personalized ads: use the opt-out links in the Advertising section.
  • Analytics: use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Emails: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email from us.
  • Your information: to ask about, correct or delete personal information we have about you, email hello@getoutofdallas.com. We may need to confirm your identity first.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under local law, such as the right to opt out of targeted advertising. We’ll respond to requests as required by the laws that apply to us.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send “Do Not Track” signals. Because there’s no common standard for responding to them, the Site doesn’t currently respond to these signals.

12Children’s privacy

The Site is for adults planning travel and isn’t directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

13Visitors outside the U.S.

The Site is operated from the United States and intended for visitors in the U.S. If you use it from somewhere else, your information may be processed in the U.S. and in other countries where our service providers operate, where privacy laws may differ from those where you live.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If you keep using the Site after an update, the updated policy applies.

15Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy? Email hello@getoutofdallas.com.

For questions about a booking, or about how a seller handles your information, contact that seller directly.
